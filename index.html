About InfuTechz

Technology Built for the Real World

InfuTechz Software Services is a technology company based in Guntur, Andhra Pradesh, India. We specialize in building intelligent, integrated systems that combine mobile applications, cloud backends, AI models, IoT hardware, and embedded electronics.
